## Issues
1. C1-C8, R1, SW1 and SW3 did not have a footprint. 
2. The transistor did not have the correct nets due to being numbered 1, 5 and 6 rather than 1, 2 and 3. 
3. 3v3 instead of +3.3V power net. 
4. Input 1,  Input2, Input 3 and Input 4 rather than b1, b2, b3 and 4.
5. U2 is not a real component.
6. SW2 pins were both GND. This has been resolved.
7. EN on the clock generator should be en. 
8. R24 and R6 do not show a connection to SH1, they are supposed to be connected.
